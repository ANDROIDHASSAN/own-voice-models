STT: tasveer se akshar

dekho: stt/model.py, stt/ctc.py · lessons 4-5
Kaam ek line mein

Har 40 millisecond ke liye batao: is pal kaunsa akshar bola ja raha hai, ya koi nahi.

Kahani

Ek clerk film ki har 4 frame mein se 1 dekhta hai (subsampling). Phir har frame ke liye do kaam: aas-paas ke frames dekhna (convolution: "ye 'k' ki shakal hai") aur poori film mein kahin bhi jhaankna (attention: "aage 'kal' aa raha hai, to ye 'k' hi hoga").

Encoder-only

LLM ek-ek token likhta tha aur peeche hi dekh sakta tha. Ye model poora clip ek saath dekhta hai, aage-peeche dono, aur saare frames ek hi baar mein nikalta hai. Isliye tez hai.

Padding ka jaal

Batch mein chhote clips ke peeche khaali jagah bharni padti hai. Agar model us khaali jagah ko "dekh" le to wahi clip akele aur batch mein alag jawab dega. check.py yahi pakadta hai.

CTC: jab koi nahi batata ki kaunsa akshar kab bola gaya

Kahani

5 second ki awaaz = 125 frames. Text = 40 akshar. Kaunsa frame kis akshar ka? Kisi ne nahi likha. CTC kehta hai: mat batao. Model har frame pe ek akshar ya "blank" bole; phir do niyam lagao: 1. lagataar dohraye akshar ek kar do, 2. blank hata do.

h h - e - l l - l - o  →  h - e - l - l - o  →  hello

Do "l" isliye bache kyunki unke beech ek blank tha. Blank na hota to niyam 1 unhe ek kar deta.

"CTC removes the need for frame-level labels. The model emits a character or a blank per frame, and the loss sums the probability of every path that collapses to the transcript, using the forward algorithm. I implemented it and it matches PyTorch's value and gradient."

TTS: akshar se awaaz

dekho: tts/ · lessons 1-9
Kaam ek line mein

Vaakya padho, tay karo har akshar kitni der bolna hai, uski tasveer (mel) banao, phir tasveer ko awaaz mein badlo.

Kahani

Gaayak ke saamne bol likhe hain. Pehle wo tay karta hai har akshar kitna kheenchna hai (duration). Phir sur aur awaaz bharta hai (decoder). Aakhir mein gala use asli dhwani banata hai (vocoder).

Saare frames ek saath

Purane models (Tacotron) ek-ek frame banate the aur kabhi shabd chhod dete ya dohrate the. Yahan durations pehle tay ho jaati hain, to chhootna ya dohrana ho hi nahi sakta, aur sab frames ek hi baar mein bante hain.

Punctuation yahan zaroori hai

STT ne comma phenk diya tha kyunki wo sunai nahi deta. TTS use rakhta hai: comma = chhota rukna, sawaaliya nishaan = uthta sur.

Har akshar kitna lamba? MAS khud dhoondhta hai

Niyam

Raasta sirf daayein ya daayein-neeche ja sakta hai: akshar kram mein, koi chhoota nahi. Jis akshar ko jitne khaane mile, wahi uski duration.

Vocoder ka kamaal

Mel mein "phase" nahi hota (lehar apne chakkar mein kahan hai). Purana tareeka (Griffin-Lim) andaza lagata hai aur jhanjhanata hai. Hamara vocoder har frame ka zor aur phase dono seekhta hai, phir ek ulta-Fourier se awaaz banti hai.

LLM  Beginner

1. LLM kya hai?

Ek machine jo "agla shabd batao" khel bahut accha khelti hai.

A neural network trained to predict the next token. Text generation is predicting, sampling and repeating.
2. Token kya hota hai?

Text ka tukda. Aam shabd poora, mushkil shabd 2-3 tukdon mein.

A subword unit. It keeps the vocabulary small while still covering any word. I built a BPE tokenizer for mine.
3. Model seekhta kaise hai?

Jawab deta hai, galti naapta hai, har knob ko thoda sahi taraf ghumata hai. Baar-baar.

Forward pass, compute the loss, backpropagate to get a gradient for every weight, and take a small step against it.
4. Embedding kya hai?

Har token ka "pata": numbers ki ek list. Milte-julte shabdon ke pate paas hote hain.

A learned vector per token. Similar tokens end up with similar vectors.
5. Temperature kya karta hai?

Kam = safe aur ek jaisa, zyada = creative par galat bhi.

It divides the logits before softmax. Low sharpens the distribution, high flattens it.

LLM  Intermediate

6. Attention kaise kaam karta hai?

Har shabd baaki shabdon ko dekhta hai aur kaam wale se jaankari le leta hai.

Each token makes a query, key and value. The query-key dot product, after softmax, weights a sum of the values.
7. Causal mask kyun?

Taaki model agla shabd dekh ke nakal na kare.

It stops a position attending to the future, so I can train on every position at once without leaking the answer.
8. Position ka pata kaise chalta hai?

Har shabd ke vector ko uski jagah ke hisaab se ghuma dete hain.

I use RoPE: queries and keys are rotated by an angle that depends on position, so attention sees relative position.
9. Overfitting kaise pakdoge?

Purane paper pe pass, naye pe fail.

Training loss keeps falling while validation loss rises. I keep the checkpoint with the best validation loss.
10. KV cache kya hai?

Purane shabdon ka hisaab yaad rakhna, taaki har baar shuru se na padhna pade.

Caching each layer's keys and values so every new token costs one position, not the whole sequence. I test that cached and uncached outputs match.

LLM  Advanced

11. Pre-norm aur RMSNorm kyun?

Deep network mein numbers ka size sambhalne ke liye, saste tareeke se.

Pre-norm keeps the residual path clean so gradients flow; RMSNorm is LayerNorm without mean subtraction, cheaper and equally stable.
12. LoRA kaise kaam karta hai?

Poori kitaab dobara nahi likhte, haashiye mein chhote notes chipkate hain.

Freeze the weights and learn a low-rank update B·A beside them. B starts at zero so step zero is the original model. I trained 0.9% of the parameters.
13. Quantization se kya khota hai?

Numbers ko chhota likhne se thodi baareeki jaati hai.

int8 per-channel cost me nothing in validation loss; at 4 bits per-channel mattered, and 2 bits broke the model. I measured each on the same batches.
14. Model galat baat kyun bolta hai?

Use sach nahi pata, sirf ye ki kaunsa shabd accha lagta hai agla.

It is trained for likely text, not true text, and has no way to verify. Grounding with retrieval is the usual fix.
15. Pehla loss kitna hona chahiye, aur kyun dekhte ho?

ln(vocabulary). Hamara 8.37 aaya, hona tha 8.32.

A random model spreads probability evenly, so the first loss should be about ln(vocab size). If it is not, the setup has a bug.

STT  Beginner

16. Awaaz computer mein kaise hoti hai?

Hawa ke dabaav ki naap, ek second mein 16,000 baar. Numbers ki lambi list.

A waveform: amplitude samples at a sampling rate. I use 16 kHz, which captures speech up to 8 kHz.
17. Spectrogram kya hai?

Awaaz ki tasveer: kab kaunsi frequency kitni zor se baji.

The magnitude of a short-time Fourier transform over time. It makes phonetic content visible, which the raw waveform does not.
18. Mel scale kyun?

Kaan neeche ki frequencies mein baareek fark sunta hai, upar nahi.

It spaces frequency bands like human hearing: fine at the bottom, coarse at the top. 80 mel bands is the standard input.
19. WER kya hai?

100 shabdon mein kitne galat.

Word error rate: the edit distance between hypothesis and reference in words, divided by the reference length. It can exceed 100%.
20. STT ko train karne ke liye kya chahiye?

Awaaz aur uska likha hua roop, saath-saath.

Paired audio and transcripts. It is supervised learning, which is why speech data is expensive.

STT  Intermediate

21. CTC kya hal karta hai?

Kaunsa frame kis akshar ka hai, ye bataye bina training.

The alignment problem. The model emits a symbol per frame, and the loss sums the probability of all paths that collapse to the transcript.
22. Blank symbol kyun?

"Abhi kuch naya nahi" kehne ke liye, aur do "l" ko alag rakhne ke liye.

It lets the model emit nothing on a frame, and it separates genuine repeated characters, which would otherwise be merged.
23. Greedy aur beam search?

Greedy sabse accha raasta leta hai; beam sabse accha text.

Greedy takes the best symbol per frame. Prefix beam search keeps the best texts and adds up the paths that collapse to each.
24. Subsampling kyun karte ho?

100 frames har second zyada hain; 25 kaafi hain aur attention sasta ho jaata hai.

Two stride-2 convolutions reduce 100 frames per second to 25. Attention cost falls 16 times and each frame covers a useful patch.
25. SpecAugment kya hai?

Training mein tasveer ki kuch pattiyan chhupa dena.

Masking random time and frequency stripes of the spectrogram. It is the main regulariser when speech data is small.

STT  Advanced

26. CTC ki kamzori kya hai?

Har frame akela faisla leta hai; use yaad nahi ki pehle kya likha.

Conditional independence between frames: no internal language model. Transducers and attention decoders remove it at the cost of speed and simplicity.
27. CTC, RNN-T aur Whisper mein fark?

CTC sabse saral, RNN-T streaming ke liye, Whisper decoder wala.

CTC is encoder-only and non-autoregressive. RNN-T adds a prediction network over past outputs. Whisper is an attention encoder-decoder, most accurate but not streaming.
28. Padding se kya bug aa sakta hai?

Batch ki khaali jagah agar model dekh le to wahi clip alag jawab dega.

If convolutions or attention read padded frames, a clip's output depends on its batch. I mask in three places and test that alone and batched outputs match.
29. Training plateau pe atki, kya kiya?

Model sirf blank bol raha tha. Warmup lamba kiya.

It was the CTC blank plateau. A controlled experiment showed the warmup was too short; with 3000 steps it left the plateau.
30. Forced alignment kya hai?

Diye hue text ke liye pata karna ki kaunsa shabd kab bola gaya.

The Viterbi version of the CTC recursion: the single best path for a known transcript. It gives word timestamps without timing labels.

TTS  Beginner

31. TTS ke do hisse kaunse hain?

Pehle text se tasveer (mel), phir tasveer se awaaz.

An acoustic model that predicts a mel spectrogram, and a vocoder that turns it into a waveform.
32. TTS, STT se mushkil kyun?

STT jaankari phenkta hai; TTS ko wo wapas banani padti hai.

It is one-to-many: one sentence can be spoken many ways, and the model must invent pitch, timing and phase.
33. Vocoder kya hai?

Mel ki tasveer se asli awaaz banane wala.

A model that generates the waveform from a mel spectrogram, which means reconstructing the phase the spectrogram dropped.
34. Duration kya hai?

Ek akshar kitni der bola jaata hai.

The number of mel frames each character or phoneme lasts. Predicting it is what lets a model generate all frames in parallel.
35. TTS mein punctuation kyun rakhte ho?

Comma rukna hai, sawaaliya nishaan uthta sur.

Punctuation carries prosody: pauses and pitch movement. Recognition drops it; synthesis needs it.

TTS  Intermediate

36. Autoregressive aur non-autoregressive TTS?

Ek-ek frame banana, ya sab ek saath.

Tacotron generates frame by frame with attention and can skip or repeat words. FastSpeech-style models fix durations first and generate in parallel.
37. MAS kya hai?

Training ke dauraan khud pata karna ki har akshar ko kitne frames mile.

Monotonic alignment search: dynamic programming for the best monotonic text-to-frame alignment, used to get durations without an external aligner.
38. Length regulator?

Har akshar ka vector utni baar dohrana jitni uski duration.

It expands the text-rate sequence to frame rate by repeating each encoder output by its duration.
39. Griffin-Lim kya hai, aur kyun kaafi nahi?

Phase ka andaza lagane ka purana tareeka; jhanjhanata hai.

An iterative phase estimate from magnitudes only. It is consistent but not natural, which is why neural vocoders exist.
40. Characters ya phonemes?

Hum seedhe akshar dete hain; Devanagari lagbhag jaisa likha waisa bola jaata hai.

I use raw characters, like AI4Bharat's Indic TTS. The cost in Hindi is schwa deletion, which the model must learn from examples.

TTS  Advanced

41. L1 loss se awaaz dhundhli kyun hoti hai?

Wo saare sambhav jawabon ka ausat banata hai.

L1 rewards the average of all plausible spectrograms, which is over-smoothed. Flow matching, diffusion or a GAN loss fix that.
42. HiFi-GAN aur Vocos mein fark?

Ek mel ko phula ke waveform banata hai, doosra frame rate pe rehke phase predict karta hai.

HiFi-GAN upsamples with transposed convolutions, so most compute is at audio rate. Vocos predicts magnitude and phase per frame and uses one inverse STFT.
43. Multi-resolution STFT loss kyun?

Chhoti window timing dekhti hai, lambi pitch.

Comparing spectrograms at several window sizes covers the time-frequency trade-off, and avoids comparing waveforms whose phases differ but sound the same.
44. Distillation kya hai, aur tumne kahan kiya?

Bada model bolta hai, chhota uski nakal seekhta hai.

Training a student on a teacher's outputs. My TTS training audio was generated by a 99 million parameter teacher; the student cannot exceed it.
45. TTS ko naapte kaise ho?

Log sunke number dete hain; ya machine se likhwa ke dekhte hain.

MOS from listeners for naturalness. For intelligibility I run the audio through a strong recognizer and compare its CER with the teacher's on the same sentences.

Orchestration  Beginner

46. Voice agent ka pipeline kya hai?

Suno, likho, socho, bolo.

VAD, speech-to-text, turn detection, an LLM, sentence chunking and text-to-speech: a cascaded pipeline.
47. VAD kya hai?

Mic pe koi bol raha hai ya nahi.

Voice activity detection: it marks where an utterance starts and ends, so the recognizer only gets speech.
48. Latency kya hai aur kyun zaroori?

User ke rukne aur agent ke bolne ke beech ka samay.

Time to first audio. Above roughly a second a conversation stops feeling natural.
49. Barge-in kya hai?

User beech mein bole to agent turant chup.

Interrupting the agent while it speaks: playback is cut and the rest of the reply is cancelled.
50. Streaming kyun?

Poore jawab ka intezaar karoge to der hogi.

Each stage starts on partial output of the one before, so the first sentence is spoken while the rest is still being generated.

Orchestration  Intermediate

51. End-of-turn detection mushkil kyun?

Beech ka rukna aur baat ka ant dono chuppi hain.

Silence alone cannot tell a pause from the end. I combine silence duration with a semantic signal from the LLM.
52. Hysteresis kya hai VAD mein?

Shuru hone aur rukne ke alag niyam, taaki faisla baar-baar na palte.

Different thresholds for starting and stopping: 100 ms of speech to start, 500 ms of silence to end, plus a pre-roll buffer.
53. Latency kahan-kahan jaati hai?

Chuppi ka intezaar, STT, LLM ka pehla tukda, TTS ka pehla vaakya.

Endpointing delay, STT time, LLM time to first token and TTS time for the first sentence. I log each per turn.
54. Model calls thread mein kyun?

Warna sochte waqt agent behra ho jaayega.

They block. Running them in worker threads keeps the event loop reading the microphone, which is what makes barge-in possible.
55. Sentence chunking kyun?

TTS ko poora vaakya chahiye, par poore jawab ka intezaar nahi.

Prosody needs whole sentences, so I regroup the token stream into sentences and synthesize each as soon as it closes.

Orchestration  Advanced

56. Cancellation sahi kaise karte ho?

Har jawab ka number; purana number wala kaam khud ruk jaata hai.

A generation counter. Threads cannot be killed, so every stage checks that its generation is still current before acting.
57. False cutoff aur latency ka trade-off?

Jaldi jawab doge to tokne ka khatra badhta hai.

A shorter endpointing delay cuts latency but raises false cutoffs. I use a short delay when the text looks finished and a long one when it does not.
58. Echo ka kya karte ho?

Speaker ki awaaz mic mein jaati hai; agent khud ko tok dega.

It needs acoustic echo cancellation. Browsers and WebRTC provide it; my local script does not, so it needs headphones.
59. WebRTC kyun, WebSocket kyun nahi?

WebRTC der se aaye packet ko chhod deta hai; WebSocket atak jaata hai.

WebRTC runs on UDP with a jitter buffer, loss concealment and echo cancellation. WebSocket is TCP, so one lost packet stalls everything behind it.
60. Orchestrator ko test kaise kiya?

Asli models ki jagah nakli functions, tay delay ke saath.

Fake models with known delays, and scenario tests: a full turn, a barge-in, a pause that is not the end, and a model that raises.
Aakhri sawaal: ye production mein chalega?

Nahi. Ye seekhne ke models hain. Sach bolna hi sahi jawab hai.

No. They are small and trained on tens of hours. The point was to understand every part; in production I would fine-tune pretrained models.
