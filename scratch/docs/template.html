Parameter kya hai?

Model ke andar ka ek knob (ek number). Training in knobs ko ghumati hai jab tak jawab sahi na aane lage. ChatGPT mein ye kharabon mein hain; hamare chaaron models milake {{P_ALL}}. Chhota hai, isliye laptop pe ban gaya aur tum har line padh sakte ho.

Candidate ka safar: ek baari, shuru se ant tak

Upar: insaan kya karta aur mehsoos karta hai. Neeche: usi pal machine ke andar kya chal raha hai.

Kadam 1-2Mic hamesha khula hai. VAD pehchanta hai ki bolna kab shuru hua aur kab ruka.
Kadam 3STT awaaz ko text banata hai. Turn detector poochta hai: baat poori hui ya beech ka rukna tha?
Kadam 4-5LLM jawab likhna shuru karta hai. Pehla vaakya bante hi TTS use bol deta hai; baaki peeche banta rehta hai.
Kadam 6Candidate beech mein bola to agent usi pal chup. Ise barge-in kehte hain.
Accha experience kya hai?

Teen cheezein: agent jaldi jawab de (kam delay), beech mein na toke (false cutoff na ho), aur tokne pe turant ruk jaaye. Teeno ek doosre se ladti hain: jaldi jawab doge to tokne ka khatra badhta hai. Chapter 7 isi santulan ke baare mein hai.

Interview mein bolo

"It is a cascaded voice pipeline: VAD, speech-to-text, a turn detector, an LLM that streams tokens, sentence chunking, and text-to-speech. The metric I optimise is time to first audio, and the agent supports barge-in."

Architecture: paanch parton mein

Upar insaan, neeche data. Har part sirf apne upar-neeche wale se baat karta hai.

"Scratch se" ka matlab

Model ka har hissa (attention, CTC ka loss, alignment, vocoder), training loop aur orchestration is folder mein haath se likha hai. PyTorch sirf tensors aur gradients ke liye hai. Koi bana-banaya model load nahi hota.

Sachchi baat

Data bahar se aaya hai (FLEURS, OpenSLR), aur TTS ne ek bade model ki awaaz ki nakal karke seekha hai (distillation). Ye model asli agent ke models ki jagah nahi lenge; inka kaam samjhana hai.

Awaaz numbers kaise banti hai

dekho: stt/audio.py · lesson 1
Kahani

Talaab mein pathar phenko to lehrein uthti hain. Awaaz bhi hawa ki lehar hai. Mic ek aisa chowkidaar hai jo har second 16,000 baar likh leta hai ki lehar abhi kitni oonchi hai. Wahi lambi list "awaaz" hai.

SampleList ka ek number: ek pal ki oonchai.
Sampling rateEk second mein kitne numbers. Sunne ke liye 16,000; bolne ke liye 22,050.
Frame25 ms ka tukda. Itni der awaaz "ek jaisi" maani jaati hai.
HopHar 10 ms pe naya frame. Yaani 100 frames har second.
Dikkat

In numbers mein "a" ya "k" kahin nahi dikhta. Swar ko swar banati hain uski frequencies: kaunse sur zor se baj rahe hain. Wo is list mein chhupi hain. Isliye tasveer badalni padti hai.

Interview mein bolo

"Audio is a sequence of samples: 16,000 per second for recognition. I parse the WAV header myself, convert to mono floats, and work on 25 millisecond frames with a 10 millisecond hop."

3

STT: tasveer se akshar

dekho: stt/model.py, stt/ctc.py · lessons 4-5
Kaam ek line mein

Har 40 millisecond ke liye batao: is pal kaunsa akshar bola ja raha hai, ya koi nahi.

Kahani

Ek clerk film ki har 4 frame mein se 1 dekhta hai (subsampling). Phir har frame ke liye do kaam: aas-paas ke frames dekhna (convolution: "ye 'k' ki shakal hai") aur poori film mein kahin bhi jhaankna (attention: "aage 'kal' aa raha hai, to ye 'k' hi hoga").

Encoder-only

LLM ek-ek token likhta tha aur peeche hi dekh sakta tha. Ye model poora clip ek saath dekhta hai, aage-peeche dono, aur saare frames ek hi baar mein nikalta hai. Isliye tez hai.

Padding ka jaal

Batch mein chhote clips ke peeche khaali jagah bharni padti hai. Agar model us khaali jagah ko "dekh" le to wahi clip akele aur batch mein alag jawab dega. check.py yahi pakadta hai.

CTC: jab koi nahi batata ki kaunsa akshar kab bola gaya

Kahani

5 second ki awaaz = 125 frames. Text = 40 akshar. Kaunsa frame kis akshar ka? Kisi ne nahi likha. CTC kehta hai: mat batao. Model har frame pe ek akshar ya "blank" bole; phir do niyam lagao: 1. lagataar dohraye akshar ek kar do, 2. blank hata do.

h h - e - l l - l - o  →  h - e - l - l - o  →  hello

Do "l" isliye bache kyunki unke beech ek blank tha. Blank na hota to niyam 1 unhe ek kar deta.

Haath se hisaab: 2 frames, har frame mein blank 0.6, "a" 0.4RaastaTextProbability
Sabse sambhav raasta "- -" hai (0.36), par sabse sambhav text "a" hai (0.64), kyunki teen raaste "a" dete hain. CTC ka loss = −ln(sahi text ke saare raaston ka jod).- -""0.36
- aa0.24
a -a0.24
a aa0.16
Interview mein bolo

"CTC removes the need for frame-level labels. The model emits a character or a blank per frame, and the loss sums the probability of every path that collapses to the transcript, using the forward algorithm. I implemented it and it matches PyTorch's value and gradient."

0.000CER, jab 16 clips ratwaye (overfit test)

Overfit test pehle, hamesha: poori training se pehle model ko sirf 16 clips ratwao. Rat le to pipeline sahi judi hai; na rate to code mein bug hai, aur koi data use theek nahi karega.

Jo galat gaya

Pehli run mein learning rate bahut jaldi poora kar diya (1000 steps mein). Model ne sabse sasta dhokha seekh liya: "hamesha blank bolo". Loss atak gaya, CER 0.78 pe. Ise blank plateau kehte hain.

Jo sahi kiya

Ek chhota experiment chalaya jisme learning rate dheere badha: 5 epoch mein CER 0.57. Saboot milne ke baad warmup 3000 steps kiya aur training dobara shuru ki. Neeli line wahi hai.

TTS: akshar se awaaz

dekho: tts/ · lessons 1-9
Kaam ek line mein

Vaakya padho, tay karo har akshar kitni der bolna hai, uski tasveer (mel) banao, phir tasveer ko awaaz mein badlo.

Kahani

Gaayak ke saamne bol likhe hain. Pehle wo tay karta hai har akshar kitna kheenchna hai (duration). Phir sur aur awaaz bharta hai (decoder). Aakhir mein gala use asli dhwani banata hai (vocoder).

Saare frames ek saath

Purane models (Tacotron) ek-ek frame banate the aur kabhi shabd chhod dete ya dohrate the. Yahan durations pehle tay ho jaati hain, to chhootna ya dohrana ho hi nahi sakta, aur sab frames ek hi baar mein bante hain.

Punctuation yahan zaroori hai

STT ne comma phenk diya tha kyunki wo sunai nahi deta. TTS use rakhta hai: comma = chhota rukna, sawaaliya nishaan = uthta sur.

Har akshar kitna lamba? MAS khud dhoondhta hai

Niyam

Raasta sirf daayein ya daayein-neeche ja sakta hai: akshar kram mein, koi chhoota nahi. Jis akshar ko jitne khaane mile, wahi uski duration.

Vocoder ka kamaal

Mel mein "phase" nahi hota (lehar apne chakkar mein kahan hai). Purana tareeka (Griffin-Lim) andaza lagata hai aur jhanjhanata hai. Hamara vocoder har frame ka zor aur phase dono seekhta hai, phir ek ulta-Fourier se awaaz banti hai.

Kitna data, aur naapa kaise

{{TTS_DATA}}

Naapne ka tareeka: hamari TTS ki awaaz ek mazboot recognizer (300 M) ko sunao aur dekho usne kitne akshar galat likhe. Wahi vaakya teacher ki awaaz mein bhi sunao. Dono ka fark = student ne kya khoya.

{{TTS_RESULTS}}
Interview mein bolo

"It is a non-autoregressive FastSpeech-style model. Durations come from monotonic alignment search during training, a duration predictor replaces it at inference, and a Vocos-style vocoder predicts magnitude and phase for an inverse STFT. The training audio was distilled from a larger teacher model."

Radd karna (cancellation)

Har jawab ka ek number hota hai. User toke to number badal jaata hai, aur purane number wala har kaam khud ruk jaata hai. Isse tooti hui reply baad mein bol nahi padti.

Sachchi baat: echo

Speaker ki awaaz mic mein wapas jaati hai, to agent khud ko sun ke khud ko tok dega. Phone aur browser ise "echo cancellation" se rokte hain. Is script mein wo nahi hai: headphone lagao.

Each reply has a generation counter, so a cancelled reply can never speak later. I test it with fake models of known delay, and end to end with the three trained models."
