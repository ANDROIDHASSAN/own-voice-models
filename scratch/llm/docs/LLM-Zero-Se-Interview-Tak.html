Kahani

Aankh pe patti baandh kar tumhe pahaad se neeche ghaati mein utarna hai. Tum pair se zameen ka dhalaan mehsoos karte ho aur neeche ki taraf ek kadam lete ho. Phir dobara. Dheere-dheere tum sabse neeche pahunch jaate ho.

Har kadam pe ek hi kaam: naya w = purana w − 0.25 × gradient. Dekho galti 9 se 0.01 tak aa gayi, sirf 5 kadam mein. Gradient minus hai to knob badhao, plus hai to ghatao.

Kadam bahut bada ho to

Tum ghaati ke paar kood jaate ho, phir wapas, har baar aur door. Galti ghatne ki jagah badhti hai. Is project mein ye sach mein hua: learning rate 0.1 pe loss infinity ho gaya, 0.05 pe theek chala. Ise divergence kehte hain.

Kadam bahut chhota ho to

Tum sahi disha mein ho par cheenti ki chaal se. Training bahut lambi ho jaati hai. Learning rate chunna ek kala hai: na bahut bada, na bahut chhota.

Par 1 crore knobs ka kya? Backpropagation

Ek knob ka dhalaan nikalna aasaan tha. Par jab crore knobs hon aur wo ek ke baad ek jude hon, to kaise pata chale ki galti mein kis knob ka kitna haath hai?

Kahani

Cricket team haar gayi. Coach aakhri over se peeche ki taraf jaata hai: "ye run kisne diye? usse pehle catch kisne chhoda?" Har khiladi ko uske hisse ki zimmedari milti hai. Backpropagation yahi hai: galti ko aakhir se shuru karke peeche ki taraf baantna.

Ab tumhe pata hai: training ke 4 kadam
  • Forward pass: input daalo, jawab nikalo.
  • Loss: jawab kitna galat hai, ek number.
  • Backward pass: har knob ka dhalaan (gradient) nikalo.
  • Update: har knob ko dhalaan ke ulte taraf thoda ghumao. Phir dobara.
Interview mein bolo

"Training minimizes a loss function with gradient descent. Backpropagation computes the gradient of the loss for every parameter by applying the chain rule backwards through the computation graph. Then each parameter moves a small step against its gradient, scaled by the learning rate."

Khud se poocho

1. Table mein kadam 1 pe w = 1.50 aur gradient = −3.00 hai. Agla w khud nikalo. 1.50 − 0.25 × (−3.00) = 1.50 + 0.75 = 2.25. Table se milao.

2. Gradient zero ho to kya matlab? Zameen seedhi hai: tum ghaati ke tal pe ho, ab knob nahi hilega.

3. Backpropagation kya nikalta hai? Har parameter ka gradient: loss mein us parameter ka kitna haath hai.

Code mein dekho

autograd.py ye sab 100 line mein, bina kisi library ke banata hai. python autograd.py chalao: wo XOR naam ki paheli seekhta hai aur loss 0.478 se 0.0000 tak jaata hai.

Haath se hisaab: softmax (scores ko chance mein badalna)

Model pehle kachche scores deta hai, jinhe logits kehte hain. Wo kuch bhi ho sakte hain: 2.0, −5, 100. Unhe chance banane ke liye teen kaam: (1) har score ka e^score nikalo, (2) sabko jodo, (3) har ek ko jod se bhaag do.

5

Attention: shabd ek doosre ko dekhte hain

seedhi 5 · query, key, value, mask · sabse zaroori seedhi
Kahani

"Raju ne aam khaya kyunki wo bhookha tha." Yahan "wo" kaun hai? Raju, aam nahi. Tumne ye kaise jaana? Tumne peeche mudkar "Raju" ko dekha. Shabd ka matlab akele nahi banta; wo doosre shabdon ko dekh kar banta hai. Attention model ko yahi "mudkar dekhna" sikhata hai.

Class wali kahani: query, key, value

Class mein har bachcha (shabd) teen cheezein rakhta hai. Query = uska sawaal ("mujhe kiski baat chahiye?"). Key = uske maathe ki parchi ("mere paas ye hai"). Value = uski asli jaankari. Jis bachche ki parchi mere sawaal se milti hai, main uski jaankari zyada leta hun.

Haath se hisaab: "billi doodh peeti"

Shabd "peeti" sochta hai: kisko dekhun? Uski query maan lo 12 hai. (Ye numbers samjhane ke liye chune hain; hisaab asli hai.)

ShabdKeyQuery · Key÷ √2Softmax = dhyaanValue
billi(2, 1)42.8311%(1, 0)
doodh(1, 3)74.9588%(0, 1)
peeti(0.5, 0.5)1.51.062%(0.5, 0.5)

Kadam 1: query aur har key ka guna karke jodo (dot product). "doodh": 1×1 + 2×3 = 7. Kadam 2: √2 se bhaag. Kadam 3: softmax, taaki sab milkar 100% ho. Kadam 4: har value ko uske % se guna karke jodo.

Nateeja: "peeti" ka 88% dhyaan "doodh" pe gaya, aur usne naya vector banaya (0.11, 0.89), jo lagbhag "doodh" ki value hai. Ab "peeti" ke andar ye jaankari hai ki kya piya ja raha hai.

Teen sawaal jo interviewer zaroor poochta hai

√ se bhaag kyun

Lambe vectors ka guna bahut bada number deta hai. Bade numbers pe softmax ek ko 100% aur baaki ko 0 de deta hai. Tab model seekhna band kar deta hai. Bhaag dene se numbers kaabu mein rehte hain.

Kai heads kyun

Ek head ek hi rishta dekh sakta hai. Hamare model mein 6 heads ek saath chalte hain: koi "kisne" dekhta hai, koi "kya", koi pichhla shabd. Phir sabka nateeja jud jaata hai.

Interview mein bolo

"In self-attention every token makes a query, a key and a value. The score between two tokens is the dot product of the query and the key, scaled by the square root of the dimension. Softmax turns scores into weights, and the output is the weighted sum of the values. A causal mask hides future tokens, and multiple heads run in parallel to capture different relations."

Khud se poocho

1. Table mein "billi" ka Query · Key khud nikalo. 1×2 + 2×1 = 4.

2. Mask hata dein to kya hoga? Model training mein agla shabd dekh kar nakal karega. Loss bahut kam dikhega par wo kuch likh nahi paayega.

3. Query, key, value mein se "asli jaankari" kaun le jaata hai? Value. Query aur key sirf ye tay karte hain ki kiski value kitni milegi.

Training: random se bhasha tak

Kahani

Ek bachche ko kahaniyon ki kitaab do aur ek khel khilao: main ek line padhunga aur ek shabd pe rukunga, tum agla shabd batao. Galat bataya to main sahi bata dunga. Laakhon baar ye khel khelne ke baad bachcha khud kahani bana lega. Kisi ne use grammar nahi sikhayi.

Sach baat: model jhooth kyun bolta hai

Dekho: Raju akela jaane ki baat karta hai, phir achanak "sabhi jaanwar utsaahit" ho jaate hain. Model ko sach nahi pata; use sirf ye pata hai ki kaunsa shabd achha lagta hai agla. Bade models mein isi ko hallucination kehte hain.

Interview mein bolo

"At inference I sample from the next-token distribution. Temperature sharpens or flattens it, top-k and top-p cut off the unlikely tail. A KV cache stores the keys and values of earlier tokens so each step only processes the new token; I tested that it gives identical logits to a full pass."

Khud se poocho

1. Bank ka chatbot bana rahe ho. Temperature kam rakhoge ya zyada? Kam. Wahan sahi aur ek jaisa jawab chahiye, creativity nahi.

2. KV cache se jawab badalta hai kya? Nahi. Sirf speed badhti hai.

Quantization: 3.14159 ko 3.1 likhna

Har knob 4 bytes leta hai. 1.2 crore knobs = 48.8 MB. Phone ke liye ye bhaari hai. To har knob ko chhote poore number (−127 se 127) mein badal dete hain, jo sirf 1 byte leta hai.

Haath se hisaab: 4 weights ko int8 banana

Sabse bada weight 0.52 hai. To scale = 0.52 ÷ 127 = 0.00409. Har weight ko scale se bhaag do aur gol kar do.

Asli nateeja

int8 tak (4 guna chhota) loss mein koi fark nahi. 4-bit pe thoda girta hai. 2-bit pe model poori tarah toot jaata hai. Yaani chhota karne ki bhi ek hadd hai.

Interview mein bolo

"For fine-tuning I used LoRA: freeze the base weights and train a small low-rank update, under one percent of the parameters. For deployment I quantized the weights to int8, which made the model four times smaller with no measurable loss; at 2 bits it collapsed."

Khud se poocho

2. LoRA mein purane weights badalte hain kya? Nahi, wo jame rehte hain. Sirf A aur B train hote hain.

Interview ke liye tayyar

Interview gyaan ka imtihaan kam, samjhane ka imtihaan zyada hai. Jo cheez tum 5 saal ke bachche ko samjha sakte ho, wo tumhe sach mein aati hai. Isliye har sawaal ke do jawab hain.

Apne project ki 2 minute ki kahani

Ye yaad nahi karna, is dhaanche pe apne shabdon mein bolna

Kyun banaya: "I was using LLMs through APIs and realized I didn't understand what happens inside. So I built one from scratch."

Kya banaya: "A 12 million parameter decoder-only transformer in PyTorch that writes Hindi, Marathi and Hinglish. I wrote every part myself: the BPE tokenizer, attention, the training loop, sampling."

Kaise: "It has 6 blocks with multi-head causal attention, RoPE for position, RMSNorm and SwiGLU. I trained it on about 11 million tokens of short stories and chat with AdamW, warmup and cosine decay."

Nateeja: "Loss went from 8.37, which is what a random model should give, down to 1.88 on held-out text. Then I added a KV cache, LoRA fine-tuning with under 1% of the weights, and int8 quantization with no loss."

Kya seekha, kya kami hai: "It writes grammatical text but not facts; it's a learning model. The biggest lesson was to test the properties, like causality and cache correctness, instead of trusting that the loss goes down."

25 sawaal: pehle simple jawab, phir interview wala

1. LLM kya hai?

Ek machine jo "agla shabd batao" khel bahut achha khelti hai.

A neural network trained to predict the next token. Generating text is predicting, sampling and repeating.
2. Parameter kya hota hai?

Machine ka ek knob. Seekhne mein yahi ghoomte hain.

A learnable number in the model. Mine has 12.2 million of them.
3. Model seekhta kaise hai?

Jawab deta hai, galti naapta hai, har knob ko thoda sahi taraf ghumata hai. Baar-baar.

Forward pass, compute the loss, backpropagate to get gradients, and update the parameters with gradient descent.
4. Backpropagation kya hai?

Galti ko aakhir se peeche ki taraf baantna, taaki har knob ko pata chale uska kitna haath tha.

The chain rule applied backwards through the computation graph to get the gradient of the loss for every parameter.
5. Learning rate kya hai? Galat ho to?

Kadam ka size. Bada ho to ghaati ke paar kood jaate ho; chhota ho to bahut der lagti hai.

The step size of each update. Too high and training diverges, too low and it is slow. I saw divergence myself when it was too high.
6. Token kya hai?

Text ka tukda. Aam shabd poora, mushkil shabd 2-3 tukdon mein.

A subword unit. It keeps the vocabulary small while still covering any word.
7. BPE kaise kaam karta hai?

Jo do tukde sabse zyada saath aate hain, unhe jod do. Phir dobara.

Start from bytes and repeatedly merge the most frequent adjacent pair until the vocabulary reaches the target size.
8. Embedding kya hai?

Har token ka "pata": 384 numbers. Milte-julte shabdon ke pate paas hote hain.

A learned vector for each token. Similar tokens end up with similar vectors.
9. Attention kya hai?

Har shabd peeche mudkar dekhta hai ki kaunsa shabd uske kaam ka hai, aur uski jaankari le leta hai.

Each token computes a weighted sum over other tokens' values, with weights from the dot product of its query and their keys, passed through softmax.
10. Query, key, value kya hain?

Query = mera sawaal. Key = tumhari parchi. Value = tumhari jaankari.

Three projections of each token. Query and key decide how much attention flows; the value is what gets passed along.
11. √d se bhaag kyun?

Warna numbers bahut bade ho jaate hain aur softmax ek hi shabd ko sab de deta hai.

Dot products grow with dimension and saturate the softmax, which kills gradients. Scaling keeps them in a healthy range.
12. Causal mask kyun?

Taaki model agla shabd dekh kar nakal na kare.

It prevents a position from attending to future tokens, so we can train on all positions at once without leaking the answer.
13. Model ko order kaise pata chalta hai?

Har shabd ke vector ko uski jagah ke hisaab se ghuma dete hain.

I use RoPE, which rotates queries and keys by an angle that depends on position, so attention scores depend on relative distance.
14. Residual connection kyun?

Har teacher copy mein note jodta hai, mitata nahi. Isse purani baat bachi rehti hai.

It gives gradients a direct path, which is what lets deep networks train. Each block only learns a correction.
15. Feed-forward layer kya karti hai?

Doosron ko dekhne ke baad har shabd akele sochta hai.

It transforms each token independently after attention has mixed information. Most of the parameters are there.
16. Loss function kya hai?

Sahi shabd ko model ne kitna chance diya, uska −ln. Kam chance = badi galti.

Cross-entropy: the negative log probability assigned to the correct next token.
17. Overfitting kya hai? Kaise pakdoge?

Ratta maarna. Purane paper pe pass, naye pe fail.

When the model memorizes training data. I detect it by comparing training loss with validation loss on held-out data.
18. Training shuru hote hi kya check karte ho?

Pehla loss ln(vocabulary) ke paas hona chahiye. Hamara 8.37 aaya, hona tha 8.32.

That the initial loss is about the log of the vocabulary size. If not, something is wrong in the model or the data.
19. Temperature kya karta hai?

Thaile ko hilata hai. Kam = safe, zyada = creative par galat bhi.

It divides the logits before softmax. Low temperature makes the distribution sharper, high makes it flatter.
20. KV cache kya hai?

Purane shabdon ka hisaab yaad rakhna, taaki har baar shuru se na padhna pade.

Caching the keys and values of previous tokens so each generation step only processes the new token.
21. Model galat baat kyun bolta hai?

Use sach nahi pata. Use bas pata hai kaunsa shabd achha lagta hai agla.

It is trained for likely text, not true text, and has no mechanism to verify facts.
22. Fine-tuning aur LoRA?

Poori kitaab dobara nahi likhte, haashiye mein chhote notes chipkate hain.

Fine-tuning adapts a pretrained model. LoRA does it by freezing the weights and training a small low-rank update.
23. Quantization?

Har number ko chhota karke likhna, jaise 3.14159 ko 3.1. Jagah bachti hai.

Storing weights in fewer bits. int8 made my model four times smaller with no loss in validation.
24. Kaise pata tumhara code sahi hai?

Maine un cheezon ke test likhe jo hamesha sach honi chahiye.

I test invariants: changing a future token must not change earlier outputs, and the KV cache must match a full forward pass.
25. Ye production mein chalega?

Nahi. Ye seekhne ka model hai. Sach bolna hi sahi jawab hai.

No. It is small and trained on little data. Its purpose was understanding; for real use I would fine-tune an existing model.
